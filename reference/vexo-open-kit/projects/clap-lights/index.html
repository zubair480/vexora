Clap lights

Clap once to switch the lamp on or off. Clap twice quickly to change its colour. The ring and the bars follow how loud the room is.

While this page is connected the band's microphone is on: its light is solid blue and locked, which is why the lamp is here on the screen. Tell the people near you. The page only gets loudness numbers; no sound is recorded and none leaves the band.

Connect does nothing? This page was opened as a file (by double-click), so the browser blocks its program. Open it through the web server on your own computer: in a terminal, in your project folder (my-project, the folder that holds vexo-open-kit), run python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1 (on Windows type python), then open http://localhost:8000/vexo-open-kit/projects/clap-lights/ (8000, or the port you started it with) in Chrome or Edge. Copied this page into your own folder? The command is the same; open http://localhost:8000/clap-lights/.

Double-tap your band, type its number and press Connect. No band? Choose "Pretend band".
