This is the live demo. Walk: the band separates walking from tool use. Speak: double-tap and say the defect. OpenAI extracts it, code checks the station against CV-102’s five stations, and a work order is drafted with an isolation note. With no station named, it asks instead of guessing. Use the tool: the technician selects the tool; on stage a phone on vibrate, labelled DEMO, stands in for a half-inch impact wrench. The band times trigger from its own sample clock. Score: 2 times a squared times T, linked to the defect and the work order. Next job: 30 minutes with a needle scaler projects about 361 points, far over the 100-point action value, so BeltGuard lists a vibration-reduced scaler (about 49 points), splitting the job, or a qualified colleague, and asks for supervisor review.
