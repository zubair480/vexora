No tool tags

The take-the-band-out test. Without the band, trigger time is a guess again, with the same 2.5 to 9 times error. The wrist is where tool use starts and stops; a phone in a pocket can’t feel it, and no fixed camera follows a technician along an overland conveyor. Hands are busy and gloved, so the report is a double-tap and speech. Because the same device hears the report and feels the tool, everything lands on one clock and links itself. And there are no RFID tags to fit to every tool: the tool comes from the job.
