Gesture trainer

Teach the band your own gestures. Press Record, do the gesture three times with a short rest in between, and do the same for the next one. After that the page names every gesture you make and the band lights up in its colour.

Connect does nothing? This page was opened as a file (by double-click), so the browser blocks its program. Open it through the web server on your own computer: in a terminal, in your project folder (my-project, the folder that holds vexo-open-kit), run python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1 (on Windows type python), then open http://localhost:8000/vexo-open-kit/projects/gesture-trainer/ (8000, or the port you started it with) in Chrome or Edge. Copied this page into your own folder? The command is the same; open http://localhost:8000/gesture-trainer/.

Double-tap your band, type its number and press Connect. No band? Choose "Pretend band".

The pretend band's light is off.
