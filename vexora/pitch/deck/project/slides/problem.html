In the UK, hand-arm vibration is counted in exposure points: 2 times the tool’s vibration squared times trigger time in hours. 100 points is the exposure action value, 400 the exposure limit value. The vibration figure comes from the tool. The time usually comes from memory, and people get it wrong: workers who were observed overestimated their tool time by a median 2.5 times, and questionnaires by a median 9 times. In the US, NIOSH estimated about 1.45 million workers use vibrating tools, and OSHA sets no limit. The products that exist add hardware: an RFID tag on every tool, a sensor glove, a separate wearable.
